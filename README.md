# Tech Retail Pricing & Profitability Strategy

An interactive Tableau analytics project exploring **pricing performance, customer profitability, and margin resilience** for a technology retailer.

## Overview

The project analyzes sales, customer, pricing, and profitability data to answer three key questions:

- Where is the business making and losing money?
- Which customers, markets, and channels drive the most profit?
- How resilient are margins to cost inflation and pricing changes?

## Key Insights

- Overall portfolio profit margin remained above **55%**, despite a significant year-over-year revenue decline.
- **Omnichannel customers generated the highest average profit per customer**, outperforming online-only and physical-only customers.
- Customer profitability varied substantially across countries, creating opportunities for more targeted market strategies.
- Product categories showed different levels of margin resilience under cost inflation, suggesting that pricing decisions should be **category-specific rather than one-size-fits-all**.
- The stress-test dashboard allows users to simulate **cost inflation and discount assumptions** and evaluate their impact on profitability.

## What I Learned

This project showed me that strong overall margins can hide significant differences across **products, customers, and channels**.

By connecting pricing performance with customer profitability and cost-risk scenarios, I was able to move beyond descriptive reporting and use analytics to support **pricing and strategic decision-making.**
